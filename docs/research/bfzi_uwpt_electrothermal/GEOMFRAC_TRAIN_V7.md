# GeomFracET V7: geometry-conditioned seawater-skin temperature enhancement

## Goal and frozen physics backbone

V6 has passed the independent R01-R03 BFZI V150 S5 9-history
physical test. Its coupled version reduced mean dynamic port-Z
relative L2 from V5's 10.137% to 7.738%, while average maximum
3-D temperature error fell from 1.664 to 1.388 K.
Its most significant remaining local temperature discrepancy is
R02 transmitting-side seawater skin at 600 s (5.642 K).

V7 retains the verified V6 physical feedback: frozen V5 SOE
impedance/32-mode heat checkpoint, geometry-dependent impedance
thermal tangent, V6 package spatial correction, and the 20%
linear seawater-skin feedback basis. The five-material energy
budget, Joule heat, nonlinear port circuit and conservation
calculations remain **unchanged**.

## Geometry/time-dependent local thermal decoder

V7 introduces a small MLP with 45 BFZI geometry/material
features, the frozen 32-mode autonomous thermal state, time,
and the left-end applied voltage. Its 16 outputs represent
zero-capacity-weighted-mean local seawater skin modes around
the TX and RX packages. The MLP outputs zero at ambient
state and shares its parameters between geometries.

Training uses 30 original BFZI S5 geometries (90 histories)
and only T04/D01-D04 (5 distinct geometries, 15 histories)
for development model selection. The student is trained
against the projection of the **physical field residual**
between BFZI V150 full-physics temperatures and the
autonomous coupled V6 prediction. Inference uses the
autonomous 32-mode state generated by V6 and reconstructs

    temp_skin_K = temp_K + Phi_seaskin16 * a_seaskin16

where the 16 columns are geometry-specific heat-capacity-
orthonormal local functions, scaled to kelvin units.
The correction is confined to the seawater volume and has
zero capacity-weighted mean. Copper and package temperature
outputs, dynamic port impedance, thermal feedback, and
physical material budgets are preserved exactly.

A separate experiment injected these nonlinear skin states
into the physical thermal feedback. Developer dynamic-Z
error rose from 7.516% (V6) to 8.395% at 20% nonlinear
feedback, while 100% nonlinear feedback reached 12.967%.
The mixed residual-feedback variants also lost impedance
accuracy. They are retained only as experimental code.
The V7 production selection therefore uses the **output-only
decoder**, leaving physically coupled V6 calculations frozen.

## Evaluation

The trained V7 output decoder selected epoch 55 of 135
optimization epochs on the held-out development trajectories.
On all 15 T04/D01-D04 development histories, the mean
maximum 3-D temperature error decreases from V6's 1.551
to V7's 1.182 K (23.8% relative reduction), with unchanged
7.516% dynamic-Z relative L2 and material budgets below 1e-8 W.

Retrospective diagnostic on the previous V6 acceptance R01-R03
(9 histories): mean maximum 3-D temperature error 1.388 to
1.226 K; R02 600-second hotspot 5.642 to 5.369 K.
These previously examined geometries were not involved in
weight fitting or checkpoint selection. Their results are
diagnostics and are not a fresh independent V7 evaluation.

The V7 configuration was frozen in
runs/geomfrac_et_v7_selection.json before computing new
S01-S04 BFZI physics trajectories. New independent cohort:
4 geometry-disjoint examples, 12 complete 60/300/600s
physical histories. S01/S02/S03 include independent scalar
parameter excursions outside some observed training
coordinate ranges; S04 tests a within-range combination.
Every geometry has passed native BFZI mesh preflight.
The independent evaluation code is
examples/bfzi_geom_v7_accept.py.

## Inference

Use the V6 physical loop and request the extra field output:

    python -m examples.bfzi_geom_neural_infer       --anchor runs/bfzi_s5_R02_ultra_v6_anchor.npz       --model runs/geomfrac_et_v4_8g_quality.pt       --tangent-model runs/geomfrac_et_v5_tangent_aware.pt       --pkg-model runs/geomfrac_et_v6_pkg.pt       --lift-model runs/geomfrac_et_v5_lift.npz       --lift-feedback --lift-gain 0.2       --skin-output-model runs/geomfrac_et_v7_skin_resid.pt       --horizon 600 --step 15 --out runs/geomfrac_et_v7_infer.npz

The saved file contains both physically coupled temp_K and
enhanced temp_skin_K. The latter remains in the same
finite-volume cell ordering as the physical BFZI head.

Results: runs/geomfrac_et_v7_skin_resid_dev.json,
runs/geomfrac_et_v7_output_dev.json,
runs/geomfrac_et_v7_output_rdiag.json, and the frozen
selection in runs/geomfrac_et_v7_selection.json.
