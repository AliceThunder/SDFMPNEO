\begin{abstract}
This document fixes the production theoretical baseline of SDF-MPNEO for underwater wireless power transfer. The retained physics are magnetoquasistatic electromagnetics and heat transfer in conductor, package and seawater. The method contains no geometry-specific impedance solver and uses no labelled full-order electromagnetic or thermal trajectories for training. A compatible tetrahedral Nedelec--P1 discretization, deterministic gauges, certified nonlinear constitutive integration, state-dependent electromagnetic energy certification, solution-data-free residual--Riesz reduction, deterministic thermal spectral reduction and an intrinsic analytic neural evolution graph form one closed method. The electromagnetic equilibrium is quasi-statically eliminated from the thermal dynamics. The network represents only the resulting continuous-time reduced flow and is trained from its exact physical residual. Arbitrary query times are evaluated directly without online thermal time stepping. Constitutive, algebraic, electromagnetic-reduction, thermal-reduction, analytic-network, spatial-mesh and outer-domain errors remain separate and are combined only through proved a posteriori bounds. Approximation orders, reduced ranks and network size are selected from engineering-output error requirements rather than empirical constants.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope, online map and non-empirical rules}

The retained physical system is
\[
\boxed{\text{magnetoquasistatic electromagnetics}\longleftrightarrow\text{heat transfer}}.
\]
The spatial domain is partitioned into
\[
\Omega(\mu_g)=\Omega_{\rm Cu}\cup\Omega_{\rm pkg}\cup\Omega_{\rm sea}\cup\Omega_{\rm passive}.
\]
Fluid velocity and pressure are outside the current model. Seawater remains an explicit electromagnetic and thermal spatial medium.

The parameter vector is
\[
\mupar=(\mu_g,\mu_u,\mu_m),
\]
where \(\mu_g\) denotes geometry, \(\mu_u\) fixed operating conditions such as frequency and port excitation, and \(\mu_m\) material parameters. The target online map is
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
(\mupar,T_0,t_q)\mapsto
\{T(\mathbf x,t_q),\mathbf Z(t_q),R(t_q),L(t_q),M(t_q),P_{\rm Cu}(t_q),P_{\rm sea}(t_q),\mathcal C(t_q)\}.
}
\label{eq:online_map}
\end{equation}
No online time marching from \(0\) to \(t_q\) is permitted in the final evolution operator.

\paragraph{Scientific rules.}
\begin{enumerate}[label=(R\arabic*)]
\item No labelled full-order electromagnetic, thermal or experimental solution trajectory trains the analytic evolution network.
\item No solved full-order state snapshot is required to construct the production electromagnetic reduced space.
\item No geometry-specific impedance solver is a core dependency.

\section{Full coupled field equations}

\subsection{Magnetoquasistatic electromagnetic problem}
For harmonic angular frequency \(\omega>0\), let
\[
\mathbf B=\nabla\times\mathbf A,
\qquad
\mathbf E=-j\omega\mathbf A-\nabla\phi,
\qquad
\mathbf J=\sigma(T)\mathbf E+\mathbf J_s.
\]
The magnetoquasistatic equations are
\begin{align}
\nabla\times\bigl(\nu\nabla\times\mathbf A\bigr)
+\sigma(T)(j\omega\mathbf A+\nabla\phi)&=\mathbf J_s,\\
\nabla\cdot\{\sigma(T)(j\omega\mathbf A+\nabla\phi)\}&=0.
\end{align}
In insulating material \(\sigma=0\); in conductor and seawater
\[
\sigma=\sigma_{\rm Cu}(T)
\quad\text{or}\quad
\sigma_{\rm sea}(T).
\]
Resolving the conductor cross-section makes skin and proximity effects part of the field solution.

\subsection{Reciprocal scaled potential}
Define
\[
\psi=\frac{\phi}{j\omega}.
\]
Then
\[
\mathbf E=-j\omega(\mathbf A+\nabla\psi).
\]
After compatible discretization and gauge elimination the electromagnetic state is
\[
\mathbf x=\begin{bmatrix}\boldsymbol\alpha\\\boldsymbol\psi\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
\mathbf A_h=R_A\boldsymbol\alpha,
\]
where \(R_A\) is the deterministic tree--cotree magnetic gauge basis. The production discrete system has the reciprocal form
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\mathbf A_{\rm em}(\aT;\mupar)\mathbf x=\mathbf b(\mupar),
\qquad
\mathbf A_{\rm em}=\mathbf K+j\mathbf D.
}
\label{eq:apsi_system}
\end{equation}
Here \(\mathbf K=\mathbf K^H\succeq0\) is the gauge-reduced magnetic curl energy and \(\mathbf D=\mathbf D^H\succeq0\) is the conductive electric energy in the scaled coordinates. After gauge removal their sum is positive definite on the physical state space.

\subsection{Joule loss and electromagnetic outputs}
Using one fixed phasor convention throughout the implementation,
\[
Q_J(\mathbf x,T)=\tfrac12\sigma(T)|\mathbf E|^2.
\]
Consequently
\begin{align}
P_{\rm Cu}&=\tfrac12\int_{\Omega_{\rm Cu}}\sigma_{\rm Cu}(T)|\mathbf E|^2\,d\Omega,\\
P_{\rm sea}&=\tfrac12\int_{\Omega_{\rm sea}}\sigma_{\rm sea}(T)|\mathbf E|^2\,d\Omega.
\end{align}
For closed impressed-current port columns \(B=[\mathbf b_1,\ldots,\mathbf b_p]\),
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\mathbf Z=j\omega B^T\mathbf A_{\rm em}^{-1}B,
\qquad
\mathbf R=\Re\mathbf Z,
\qquad
\mathbf L=\frac{\Im\mathbf Z}{\omega}.
}
\label{eq:multiport_z}
\end{equation}
Mutual inductance is an off-diagonal entry of \(\mathbf L\). Reciprocity follows from the complex-symmetric field operator; \(\mathbf Z\) is not symmetrized after the solve.

\subsection{Thermal problem}
The temperature satisfies
\begin{equation}
\rho c_p\partial_tT-\nabla\cdot(k\nabla T)
=Q_J(\mathbf x,T;\mupar)
\quad\text{in }\Omega(\mu_g),
\label{eq:heat_full}
\end{equation}
with physical boundary conditions and continuity of temperature and heat flux across perfect interfaces. Contact resistance is included only when it is a declared physical parameter. Spatial truncation and open/infinite-domain errors remain explicit certificate terms.

\subsection{Quasi-static electromagnetic closure}
The model assumes a verified scale separation
\[
\tau_{\rm em}\ll\tau_T.
\]
Electromagnetics is therefore an instantaneous algebraic equilibrium at each thermal state. This is not one-way decoupling: temperature changes conductivity, conductivity changes fields and loss, and loss drives temperature.

\section{Compatible tetrahedral spatial discretization}

\subsection{Discrete de Rham structure}
On a conforming tetrahedral complex let \(G\) be the node-to-edge gradient incidence and \(C\) the edge-to-face curl incidence. They satisfy the exact topological identity
\begin{equation}
\boxed{CG=0.}
\label{eq:cg0}
\end{equation}
First-order Nedelec edge functions represent the magnetic vector potential and P1 nodal functions represent temperature and conducting-region scalar potential. The same tetrahedral material partition is shared by electromagnetic and thermal operators.

\subsection{Gauge elimination}
A deterministic spanning tree fixes magnetic gradient freedom. Tree-edge magnetic coefficients are removed and cotree edges form \(R_A\). In each connected conducting component one scalar-potential reference node is removed. Gauge freedom is therefore eliminated structurally; no penalty or fitted gauge parameter is introduced.

\subsection{Geometry charts}
For a continuous geometry family, a certified chart uses a non-degenerate map
\[
F_{\mu_g}:\widehat\Omega\rightarrow\Omega(\mu_g),
\]
with
\[
0<J_-\le |\det DF_{\mu_g}|\le J_+,
\qquad
\norm{DF_{\mu_g}}+\norm{DF_{\mu_g}^{-1}}\le C_F.
\]
Thermal fields use the \(H^1\) pullback and electromagnetic vector fields the covariant Piola transform. A topology change defines a different chart. The current production code already supports unstructured tetrahedra for one chart; certified multi-geometry chart transport remains an implementation obligation.

\section{Certified nonlinear constitutive integration}

\subsection{Copper law}
For copper with linear resistivity
\[
\rho_{\rm Cu}(T)=\rho_{\rm ref}[1+\alpha(T-T_{\rm ref})],
\]
the conductivity is
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\sigma_{\rm Cu}(T)=\frac{\sigma_{\rm ref}}{1+\alpha(T-T_{\rm ref})}.
}
\label{eq:copper_sigma}
\end{equation}
Inside one P1 tetrahedron the denominator \(d(x)\) is affine. Let
\[
\bar d=\frac{d_{\max}+d_{\min}}{2},
\qquad
z=\frac{d}{\bar d}-1,
\qquad
|z|\le q<1.
\]
Then
\begin{align}
\frac1d&=\frac1{\bar d}\sum_{n=0}^{N}(-z)^n+R_N,\\
\frac1{d^2}&=\frac1{\bar d^2}\sum_{n=0}^{N}(n+1)(-z)^n+R_N^{(2)},
\end{align}
with certified relative bounds
\begin{align}
\frac{|R_N|}{|1/d|}&\le q^{N+1},\\
\frac{|R_N^{(2)}|}{|1/d^2|}&\le q^{N+1}[(N+2)+(N+1)q].
\end{align}
The smallest \(N\) satisfying the allocated constitutive error is used.

\subsection{Exact tetrahedral polynomial integration}
For barycentric coordinates \(\lambda_i\),
\begin{equation}
\int_T\prod_{i=1}^4\lambda_i^{\alpha_i}\,dV
=6|T|\frac{\prod_i\alpha_i!}{(3+\sum_i\alpha_i)!}.
\label{eq:barycentric_integral}
\end{equation}
All retained constitutive polynomial terms are therefore integrated exactly against Nedelec basis functions. The same material representation is used for \(\mathbf A_{\rm em}\), its thermal derivatives, loss operators and loss derivatives.

\section{Physical electromagnetic energy theorem}

For one thermal/parameter state define
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\mathbf H(\etaPar)=\mathbf K(\etaPar)+\mathbf D(\etaPar).
}
\label{eq:physical_H}
\end{equation}
It induces
\[
\norm{\mathbf v}_{H}^2=\mathbf v^H\mathbf H\mathbf v,
\qquad
\norm{\mathbf r}_{H^{-1}}^2=\mathbf r^H\mathbf H^{-1}\mathbf r.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Contrast-independent electromagnetic coercivity]
For \(\mathbf A_{\rm em}=\mathbf K+j\mathbf D\) with \(\mathbf K,\mathbf D\succeq0\) and \(\mathbf H=\mathbf K+\mathbf D\succ0\),
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
|\mathbf v^H\mathbf A_{\rm em}\mathbf v|
\ge\frac1{\sqrt2}\mathbf v^H\mathbf H\mathbf v.
}
\label{eq:energy_coercivity}
\end{equation}
Hence the physical energy stability constant satisfies
\[
\boxed{\beta_H\ge1/\sqrt2}
\]
independently of the copper/seawater conductivity ratio.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Set \(k=\mathbf v^H\mathbf K\mathbf v\ge0\) and \(d=\mathbf v^H\mathbf D\mathbf v\ge0\). Then
\[
|\mathbf v^H\mathbf A_{\rm em}\mathbf v|=\sqrt{k^2+d^2}
\ge(k+d)/\sqrt2.
\]
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Residual-to-state bound]
If \(\widetilde{\mathbf x}\) has residual \(\mathbf r=\mathbf b-\mathbf A_{\rm em}\widetilde{\mathbf x}\), then
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\norm{\mathbf x-\widetilde{\mathbf x}}_H
\le\sqrt2\,\norm{\mathbf r}_{H^{-1}}.
}
\label{eq:residual_state_bound}
\end{equation}
\end{corollary}

This state-dependent physical metric is the production error metric. A fixed reference metric \(H_0=H(0)\) is used only to provide stable coordinates for one global reduced basis; it does not replace the local error theorem.

\section{Certified Riesz actions and scalable dual norms}

The production method does not require an exact global \(H^{-1}\). Let
\[
\mathbf y=\mathbf H^{-1}\mathbf r,
\qquad
\widetilde{\mathbf y}\approx\mathbf y.
\]
A certified Riesz action returns \(\widetilde{\mathbf y}\) and \(\delta\) such that
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\norm{\mathbf y-\widetilde{\mathbf y}}_H\le\delta.
}
\label{eq:riesz_action_error}
\end{equation}
Therefore
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\max(0,\norm{\widetilde{\mathbf y}}_H-\delta)
\le\norm{\mathbf r}_{H^{-1}}
\le\norm{\widetilde{\mathbf y}}_H+\delta.
}
\label{eq:dual_interval}
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[Certified inexact Riesz action]
Let \(P\succ0\) be a preconditioner satisfying
\[
H\succeq mP,
\qquad m>0.
\]
For an approximate Riesz solution \(\widetilde y\) with action residual \(s=r-H\widetilde y\),
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\norm{H^{-1}r-\widetilde y}_H^2
\le\frac1m s^HP^{-1}s.
}
\label{eq:pcg_action_bound}
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}
Thus Krylov iteration stops only after an engineering error requirement or a dual-norm threshold has been proved. An internal relative residual tolerance is not a scientific parameter.

\section{Physical block Riesz theorem}

Partition the energy metric into gauge-reduced magnetic and conductive scalar coordinates:
\[
H=
\begin{bmatrix}
K_A+D_{AA}&D_{A\psi}\\
D_{\psi A}&D_{\psi\psi}
\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Suppose
\begin{equation}
D_{AA}\preceq\gamma K_A.
\label{eq:gamma_domination}
\end{equation}
Positivity of the conductive block gives a Cauchy--Schwarz relation for the off-diagonal coupling. The optimal Young inequality yields
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
H\succeq m_{\rm phys}(\gamma)\diag(K_A,D_{\psi\psi}),
}
\label{eq:block_bound}
\end{equation}
where
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
m_{\rm phys}(\gamma)=
\frac{2}{2+\gamma+\sqrt{\gamma^2+4\gamma}}.
}
\label{eq:mphys}
\end{equation}
If certified block actions satisfy
\[
K_A\succeq m_KP_K,
\qquad
D_{\psi\psi}\succeq m_EP_E,
\]
then
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
H\succeq
m_{\rm phys}(\gamma)\min(m_K,m_E)\diag(P_K,P_E).
}
\label{eq:block_action_bound}
\end{equation}
This is the scalability contract: magnetic and scalar block solvers may be replaced independently as long as their spectral constants are proved.

\subsection{Magnetic face-circulation auxiliary space}
Let \(C\) be the exact edge-to-face incidence and define the gauge-restricted face circulation
\[
y=CR_A\alpha.
\]
For a tetrahedron \(T_q\), the first-order Nedelec curl \(c_q=\nabla\times A_h|_{T_q}\) is constant. For selected faces assigned to that tetrahedron define
\[
B_q=
\begin{bmatrix}
|f_1|n_1^T\\
\vdots\\
|f_m|n_m^T
\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
y_q=B_qc_q.
\]
If \(B_q\) has full row rank,
\[
\norm{c_q}^2\ge y_q^T(B_qB_q^T)^{-1}y_q.
\]
Hence
\begin{equation}
\nu_q|T_q|\norm{c_q}^2
\ge y_q^T\left[\nu_q|T_q|(B_qB_q^T)^{-1}\right]y_q.
\end{equation}
Assign each selected global face to one adjacent tetrahedron and assemble the corresponding exact integer topology row matrix \(S\) from \(CR_A\). Then
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
P_A=S^TWS\preceq K_A.
}
\label{eq:face_aux_bound}
\end{equation}
Therefore the magnetic lower spectral constant is exactly
\[
\boxed{m_K=1.}
\]
The selected square row set must be proved nonsingular using exact topological/integer rank logic, not a floating magnitude threshold. Its inverse action may be organized by topology-derived blocks or a certified multilevel auxiliary solve. A construction that collapses to the entire magnetic space is correctness-only and is not called scalable.

\subsection{Factorization-free coupling bound}
Because \(P_A\preceq K_A\),
\[
K_A^{-1}\preceq P_A^{-1}.
\]
For \(D_{AA}\succeq0\),
\begin{equation}
\gamma
\le\tr(K_A^{-1}D_{AA})
\le\tr(P_A^{-1}D_{AA}).
\label{eq:gamma_trace_aux}
\end{equation}
Thus a certified auxiliary action for \(P_A^{-1}\) gives a factorization-free upper bound for \(\gamma\), with its numerical residual propagated into the trace bound. No diagonal shift is permitted when a certificate fails.

\section{Solution-data-free sparse electromagnetic reduction}

Define the complete electromagnetic parameter
\[
\etaPar=(\mu_g,\mu_u,\mu_m,\aT)\in\mathcal D_{\rm em}.

Let \(V_n\) be the current global basis. At a parameter/state point, the production reduced equilibrium is the Galerkin system
\begin{equation}
A_r(\etaPar)c=V_n^H b,
\qquad

\qquad

\label{eq:em_rom}
\end{equation}
Only \(A_r\) is dense. Full-order operators remain sparse.

The sparse full residual is
\[
r(\etaPar)=b-A_{\rm em}(\etaPar)x_r.

Using the local physical metric,
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\Delta_{\rm em}(\etaPar)
=\sqrt2\,\overline{\norm{r(\etaPar)}_{H(\etaPar)^{-1}}}
}
\label{eq:em_rom_certificate}
\end{equation}
is a certified electromagnetic state-error upper bound, where the overline denotes the upper endpoint returned by a certified Riesz action.

\subsection{Residual--Riesz enrichment without solution snapshots}

and append it to the span. No full-order state \(A_{\rm em}^{-1}b\) is used as a basis snapshot.

and whiten

\]
The error certificate nevertheless always uses the local \(H(\etaPar)\).

\subsection{Joint multiport reduction}
All unit-port source columns are included in one residual-greedy construction. At a thermal state,

A_rC=V^HB,
\qquad

\qquad

\subsection{Finite-set versus continuous-domain certification}

For affine families this may use branch-and-bound with exact derivative/Lipschitz bounds. \section{Reduced losses and electromagnetic--thermal closure}

For thermal test mode \(k\), let \(H_k^{\rm Cu}(\aT)\) and \(H_k^{\rm sea}(\aT)\) be the reduced quadratic loss operators assembled from the same constitutive representation used by the field equation. Then
\begin{equation}
[q_{\rm em,r}]_k
=\Re\left\{c^H[H_k^{\rm Cu}(\aT)+H_k^{\rm sea}(\aT)]c\right\}.
\label{eq:reduced_heat_source}
\end{equation}
No full three-dimensional loss field is reconstructed online.

Differentiating the reduced electromagnetic equilibrium gives
\[
A_r\frac{\partial c}{\partial a_j}
=-\frac{\partial A_r}{\partial a_j}c
+\frac{\partial b_r}{\partial a_j}.
\]
Hence
\begin{align}
\frac{\partial q_k}{\partial a_j}
={}&2\Re\left[\left(\frac{\partial x_r}{\partial a_j}\right)^HH_kx_r\right]
+\Re\left[x_r^H\frac{\partial H_k}{\partial a_j}x_r\right].
\label{eq:heat_jacobian}
\end{align}
The production impressed-current model has \(\partial b_r/\partial a_j=0\); other physical port models may add the explicit source derivative.

\section{Deterministic thermal spectral reduction}

After boundary lifting, solve
\begin{equation}
K_T\phi_i=\lambda_iM_T\phi_i,
\qquad

\label{eq:thermal_eig}

Let
\[

\label{eq:thermal_tail}
\end{equation}
The smallest \(r\) satisfying the propagated output requirement is retained. A production partial eigensolver must also certify a lower bound for \(\lambda_{r+1}\); computing the full spectrum is a correctness implementation, not the final scalable method.

For mass-normalized retained coordinates,
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\dot a+\Lambda_Ta=g_{\rm em}(a;\mupar)+f_T(\mupar).

\label{eq:closed_reduced}
\end{equation}
This is the exact reduced electromagnetic--thermal dynamics up to the separately certified spatial, constitutive and reduction errors.

\section{Intrinsic analytic neural evolution operator}

\subsection{Analytic algebra}
For one fixed operator chart with decay vector \(\lambda\), define
\[
\mathscr A_\lambda
=\operatorname{span}\{t^me^{-\rho t}:m\in\mathbb N_0,\ \rho=n^T\lambda,\ n\in\mathbb N_0^r\}.
\]
The algebra is closed under addition, multiplication, differentiation and the stable response operator \((\partial_t+\lambda_i)^{-1}\).

Initial-state and operating-condition nodes are zero-dynamics analytic nodes. Thus one chart represents
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
(a_0,U,t)\mapsto a_\theta(t)
}
\label{eq:parametric_analytic_map}
\end{equation}
inside the network itself; no external conditioning network generates weights.

\subsection{Analytic neuron}
A neuron is the exactly solvable dissipative response
\begin{equation}
(\partial_t+\lambda_i)h_\nu=S_\nu,
\qquad
h_\nu(0)=h_{\nu,0},
\label{eq:analytic_neuron}
\end{equation}
where \(S_\nu\) is an algebraic combination of parameter nodes, initial-state nodes and preceding analytic neurons. Therefore
\[
h_\nu(t)=e^{-\lambda_it}h_{\nu,0}
+\int_0^te^{-\lambda_i(t-\tau)}S_\nu(\tau)\,d\tau.
\]
Network depth represents nonlinear interaction order, not time steps.

\subsection{Stable evaluation}
A fast compiler reduces a finite graph to unique polynomial--exponential atoms. An independent exact realization writes each scalar graph output as
\begin{equation}
\boxed{h(t)=c^Te^{At}b.}
\label{eq:state_space_realization}
\end{equation}
Repeated and nearly repeated decay rates are represented by ordinary Jordan/confluent structure; no near-resonance threshold is introduced. Large realizations may be compressed only with a certified output/residual error bound.

\section{Solution-data-free closed-loop training}

The analytic graph supplies both \(a_\theta\) and \(\dot a_\theta\) exactly. The true training residual is the closed electromagnetic--thermal residual
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
R_\theta(a_0,U,t)
=\dot a_\theta+\Lambda_Ta_\theta
-g_{\rm em}(a_\theta;U)-f_T(U).
}
\label{eq:nn_residual}
\end{equation}
The call to \(g_{\rm em}\) uses the actual reduced electromagnetic equilibrium, nonlinear material law and reduced Joule source; it is not replaced by labelled trajectories. The primary objective is the natural reduced thermal energy/dual norm integrated or maximized over the certified initial-state/operating/time domain with deterministic error-controlled quadrature or branch-and-bound.

\subsection{Residual-grown topology}
Network width and depth are not prescribed. For a candidate response \(h\) targeting thermal coordinate \(i\), let its source satisfy
\[
(\partial_t+\lambda_i)h=\psi.
\]
The first variation of the physical residual is
\begin{equation}
D=e_i\psi-J_g(a_\theta)e_i h,
\qquad
J_g=\frac{\partial g_{\rm em}}{\partial a}.
\label{eq:candidate_direction}
\end{equation}
For the selected deterministic quadrature/inner product, the tangent optimal candidate weight is
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
w^*=-\frac{\ip{R_\theta}{D}}{\ip{D}{D}}.
}
\label{eq:candidate_weight}
\end{equation}
The candidate is accepted only after recomputing the complete nonlinear residual. A tangent prediction alone is never a certificate.

Candidate dictionaries grow hierarchically from active response nodes, initial-state nodes and static parameter nodes. Global growth terminates only when the propagated engineering-output certificate is met or the domain is declared uncertified.

\section{Stability and unified electromagnetic--thermal certificate}

For the exact reduced vector field
\[
F(a;\mupar)=-\Lambda_Ta+g_{\rm em}(a;\mupar)+f_T(\mupar),
\]
define
\begin{equation}
\kappa=
\lambda_{\min}(\Lambda_T)
-\sup_{(a,\mupar)\in\mathcal D}
\lambda_{\max}\left[\sym\left(\frac{\partial g_{\rm em}}{\partial a}\right)\right].
\label{eq:kappa}
\end{equation}
If a verified lower bound proves \(\kappa>0\), the reduced dynamics is contractive on the certified domain.

Keep the error causes separate:
\begin{align*}
\eta_{\rm constitutive}&:\ \text{material-series/tabulation remainder},\\
\eta_{\rm algebraic}&:\ \text{certified linear/Riesz action error},\\
\eta_{\rm EM-ROM}&:\ \text{electromagnetic reduced-state residual error},\\
\eta_{\rm mesh}&:\ \text{spatial discretization error},\\
\eta_{\rm outer}&:\ \text{open/infinite-domain truncation error},\\
\eta_{\rm T-ROM}&:\ \text{thermal spectral tail},\\
\eta_{\rm NN}&=\norm{R_\theta}:\ \text{analytic evolution residual}.
\end{align*}
Electromagnetic errors are first propagated to a reduced heat-source bound \(\eta_Q\). Define
\[
\eta_{\rm total}=\eta_{\rm T-ROM}+\eta_Q+\eta_{\rm NN}.
\]
For exact initial-condition embedding and \(\kappa>0\),
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\norm{a_\theta(t)-a(t)}
\le\int_0^te^{-\kappa(t-s)}\eta_{\rm total}(s)\,ds
\le\frac{\sup_{s\le t}\eta_{\rm total}(s)}{\kappa}.
}
\label{eq:uniform_error}
\end{equation}
If \(\kappa\le0\), only a finite-time Gronwall bound is returned.

For any differentiable engineering output \(Q\),
\[
|Q-Q_\theta|
\le L_{Q,T}\eta_T+L_{Q,u}\eta_{\rm em}
+\eta_{Q,{\rm constitutive}}+\eta_{Q,{\rm algebraic}}
+\eta_{Q,{\rm mesh}}+\eta_{Q,{\rm outer}}.
\]
The user specifies accuracy on outputs such as \(T_{\max},Z,R,L,M,P_{\rm Cu},P_{\rm sea}\); internal ranks, constitutive orders and network size are then chosen to satisfy that budget.
