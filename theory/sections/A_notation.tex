The vNext convention is
\[
  f_{\rm phys}(t)=\Rea\{\widetilde f e^{+j\omega t}\},
\]
with peak rather than RMS phasors. Therefore average port power is
\[
  P=\frac12\Rea\{v\adj i\}.
\]
Constitutive signs, Green kernels, Maxwell operators, and loss formulas must be
implemented consistently with this convention. Physical passivity is verified
through non-negative time-averaged power and structured passive realizations.

A superscript $\adj$ denotes conjugate transpose, while $\T$ denotes ordinary
transpose. $\Herm A=(A+A\adj)/2$. Positive-semidefinite and
positive-definite matrices are denoted by $\PSD$ and $\SPD$, respectively.
