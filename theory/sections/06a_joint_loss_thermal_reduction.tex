\section{Joint reduction of electromagnetic loss and thermal response}
\label{sec:joint-loss-thermal}

Reducing the electromagnetic loss field and the thermal dynamics independently
can waste rank on heat patterns that are electromagnetically distinct but
thermally indistinguishable. vNext therefore permits a joint,
observable-aware reduction.

\subsection{Fine loss dictionary}

Let a high-fidelity electromagnetic teacher produce a dictionary of PSD loss
patterns
\[
  q(\mathbf r;u)
  \approx
  \sum_{k=1}^{K_q}\alpha_k(u)\Psi_k^{\rm fine}(\mathbf r),
\]
where $u$ spans port currents, frequencies, temperatures, and representative
scenes.

The dictionary is not exposed online. It is an offline object used to discover
thermal source channels.

\subsection{Thermal transfer metric}

Let $\mathcal H_T$ map a heat source to the set of thermal observables over the
declared time window. Define the source-response inner product
\[
 \inner{q_1}{q_2}_{\mathcal H}
 =
 \int_{\Gamma_s}
 w(s)
 \inner{\mathcal H_T(s)q_1}
        {\mathcal H_T(s)q_2}\,\dd\mu(s),
\]
where $\Gamma_s$ is a set/contour of Laplace samples and $w$ selects relevant
time scales and observations.

Two heat patterns that differ strongly in $L^2(\Omega)$ but have nearly the same
thermal response are close in this metric.

\subsection{Thermally observable source basis}

The reduced heat basis
\[
 \{\Psi_\ell\}_{\ell=1}^{R_q}
\]
is obtained by a POD/SVD/eigendecomposition in the transfer metric above, or by
an equivalent source-side controllability/observability construction. The rank
is selected so
\[
 \epsilon_{\rm source\to T}
 =
 \sup_{q\in\mathcal Q_{\rm val}}
 \frac{
  \norm{\mathcal H_T(q-\Pi_q q)}_{\mathcal Y}
 }{
  \max(\norm{\mathcal H_Tq}_{\mathcal Y},\epsilon_T)
 }
 \le \tau_q .
\]
Thus source rank is tied directly to temperature accuracy.

\subsection{Preserving local positivity}

An observable-optimal linear basis may contain signed fields. The online loss
decoder therefore retains a PSD/non-negative fine factorization and maps it to
source amplitudes through an energy-preserving aggregation:
\[
  p_\ell
  =
  \int w_\ell(\mathbf r)q(\mathbf r)\dd V,
  \qquad
  w_\ell(\mathbf r)\ge0
\]
when strict channel positivity is required. More general signed latent channels
are allowed internally only if the continuous reconstructed physical heat field
remains non-negative and integrated power closes exactly.

\subsection{Joint source/state realization}

A stronger reduction forms one coupled realization
\[
  C_T\dot z=-G_Tz+\widetilde B_T a_q,
\]
where $a_q$ are coefficients of a compact PSD loss factor. Source basis and
thermal state basis are optimized together to minimize held-out temperature
observable error.

\subsection{Geometry dependence}

Source modes are object-local and transported with geometry. A conductor
surface/volume heat pattern follows its conductor pose automatically; only its
intrinsic profile and modal amplitude change. This avoids relearning a moving
heat source in world coordinates.

\subsection{Certification}

The release suite reports both
\[
  \epsilon_q
  \quad\text{and}\quad
  \epsilon_{q\to T}.
\]
A relatively large pointwise loss-field error may be acceptable only if the
declared product does not require that field and the thermally observable error
is below budget. If the API exposes spatial Joule density as an engineering
output, its separate spatial error budget remains mandatory.
