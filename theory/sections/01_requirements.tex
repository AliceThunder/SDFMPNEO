\section{System requirements and non-negotiable invariants}

Let a scene be denoted by
\[
  \mathcal S=(\mathcal G,\mathcal M,\mathcal B),
\]
where $\mathcal G$ is the set of geometric objects and their poses,
$\mathcal M$ their constitutive laws, and $\mathcal B$ the electrical and
thermal environment specification. The online model accepts
\[
  (\mathcal S,\,u(t),\,T_0)
\]
and returns electrical port quantities, continuous loss queries, and
temperature evolution.

\subsection{Required geometric scope}

The theory shall support:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(G\arabic*)]
\item a variable number of conducting coils and passive bodies;
\item independent arbitrary poses in $\SE(3)$;
\item superelliptic spiral centerlines and superelliptic conductor cross-sections;
\item independently variable conductor width, thickness, pitch, turn count,
      superellipse exponent, and planar aspect ratio;
\item arbitrary package dimensions and poses, including superquadrics and
      constructive combinations of primitives;
\item piecewise homogeneous, layered, continuously inhomogeneous, and
      anisotropic material regions.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Required electromagnetic invariants}

For a reciprocal $P$-port model with phasor current $i\in\C^P$,
\[
  v=Z(\omega;\mathcal S)i .
\]
The decoded impedance must satisfy reciprocity
\[
  Z^\T=Z
\]
when the constitutive scene is reciprocal. The dissipative Hermitian part
must satisfy
\[
  R(\omega;\mathcal S)
  :=\Herm Z
  =\frac{Z+Z\adj}{2}\PSD .
\]
If $D_{\mathrm{vol}}$ denotes internally absorbed electromagnetic power and
$D_{\mathrm{out}}$ an outward/radiative channel, then
\[
  R=D_{\mathrm{vol}}+D_{\mathrm{out}},\qquad
  D_{\mathrm{vol}}\PSD,\quad D_{\mathrm{out}}\PSD .
\]

\subsection{Required thermal invariants}

The reduced thermal dynamics must be representable as
\[
  C_T(z,\mathcal S)\dot z
  =-G_T(z,\mathcal S)z+B_T(z,\mathcal S)p(t)+f_{\mathrm{env}},
\]
with
\[
  C_T=C_T^\T\SPD,\qquad G_T=G_T^\T\PSD
\]
for passive conduction and convection. The temperature decoder must reproduce a
uniform reference state exactly and must not produce unbounded temperature
growth under zero input in a passive environment.

\subsection{Accuracy modes}

The public model exposes three conceptual accuracy levels:
\begin{description}
\item[FAST] no full electromagnetic solve; analytic baseline plus neural
  operators only;
\item[CERTIFIED] FAST prediction followed by physical residual evaluation and a
  bounded number of matrix-free corrections;
\item[REFERENCE] high-accuracy teacher solution used offline for dataset
  generation, release validation, and scientific benchmarking.
\end{description}

The same continuous scene semantics are used in all three modes.
