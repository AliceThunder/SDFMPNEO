\section{Complexity model and reusable physical caches}

\subsection{Complexity variables}

The relevant problem sizes are:
\[
 N_c=\text{conductor modal unknowns},\quad
 N_s=\text{surface unknowns},\quad
 N_v=\text{local contrast unknowns},
\]
\[
 P=\text{port count},\quad
 R_q=\text{loss-channel rank},\quad
 R_T=\text{thermal-state rank}.
\]
The background volume is deliberately absent from this list.

\subsection{REFERENCE electromagnetic cost}

With fast kernel application,
\[
 N=N_c+N_s+N_v,
\]
and one operator application should scale approximately as
\[
 O(N\log N)
\]
or better in favorable geometries. The total solve cost is this application
cost times the preconditioned iteration count. The research objective is to
keep that iteration count weakly dependent on geometric scale and material
contrast through physics-based block preconditioning.

\subsection{FAST cost}

The scene encoder cost scales with object/interaction graph size, while the
structured port decode is approximately
\[
 O(P^3)
\]
for dense circuit solution and much less for small fixed $P$. Loss and
temperature fields cost
\[
 O(R_q N_{\rm query})
 \quad\text{and}\quad
 O(R_T N_{\rm query})
\]
respectively, so spatial resolution is paid only when queried.

\subsection{CERTIFIED cost}

CERTIFIED mode reuses FAST predictions and cached physical blocks. If $m$ is
the correction Krylov count, incremental electromagnetic cost is roughly
\[
 m\,C_{\rm op}(N)
\]
plus residual and dual evaluations. The central performance metric is therefore
the empirical distribution of $m$, not only raw neural latency.

\subsection{Intrinsic self-block cache}

For a rigid conductor in a homogeneous isotropic medium, its self operator
depends on intrinsic geometry, material, frequency, temperature, and basis
policy but not absolute pose. Cache key:
\[
 K_{\rm self}
 =
 H(\text{intrinsic geometry},\text{material},\omega,T,\text{basis policy}).
\]

\subsection{Pair-interaction cache}

Pair blocks depend on intrinsic object identities and relative pose:
\[
 K_{ij}
 =
 H(K_i,K_j,R_i^\T R_j,R_i^\T(t_j-t_i),
   \text{environment kernel}).
\]
For optimization trajectories, nearby relative poses may reuse hierarchical
interpolation or low-rank updates.

\subsection{Thermal cache}

Thermal intrinsic blocks and Green responses are cached by material law,
intrinsic geometry, and thermal boundary class. Pair/interface transfer blocks
use relative pose. Neural checkpoints and release certificates are separate
cache layers and cannot invalidate these physical caches.

\subsection{Cache correctness}

Every cache hit must be verifiable by reconstructing the minimal physical
identity. Tolerance changes may invalidate a numerical approximation level
without invalidating a more accurate stored primitive. Caches are monotone in
accuracy when possible: a stricter query can refine an existing entry rather
than discard it.
