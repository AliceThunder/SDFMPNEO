\section{Electromagnetic teacher as a hybrid integral operator}

\subsection{Motivation}

The reference electromagnetic formulation should avoid discretizing large
volumes of homogeneous background material. Infinite-space or layered-medium
Green functions satisfy radiation conditions analytically; only conductors,
material interfaces, and truly inhomogeneous contrast regions require numerical
degrees of freedom.

\subsection{Current--charge conductor representation}

For conductor currents,
\[
 \mathbf E(\mathbf r)
 =-j\omega\mathbf A(\mathbf r)-\nabla\phi(\mathbf r),
\]
with
\[
 \mathbf A(\mathbf r)
 =\mu\int_{\Omega_c}
   G_k(\mathbf r,\mathbf r')\mathbf J(\mathbf r')\dd V',
\]
\[
 \phi(\mathbf r)
 =\frac{1}{\epsilon}
  \int_{\Omega_c}
  G_k(\mathbf r,\mathbf r')\rho(\mathbf r')\dd V',
\]
and charge continuity
\[
  \nabla\cdot\mathbf J=-j\omega\rho.
\]
In a homogeneous background,
\[
 G_k(\mathbf r,\mathbf r')
 =\frac{\exp(-jk\norm{\mathbf r-\mathbf r'})}
        {4\pi\norm{\mathbf r-\mathbf r'}}.
\]

The conductor current is expanded in a tensor-product modal basis:
\[
 \mathbf J(s,\xi_1,\xi_2)
 =
 \sum_{\ell=1}^{N_s}\sum_{m=1}^{N_\perp}
 a_{\ell m}\,\chi_\ell(s)\bm\psi_m(\xi_1,\xi_2).
\]
The cross-section basis explicitly represents uniform, skin, edge, and
proximity modes. Increasing $N_\perp$ refines conductor physics without
refining the surrounding world.

\subsection{Dielectric and magnetic interfaces}

For piecewise homogeneous passive bodies, surface-equivalence formulations
introduce electric and magnetic surface currents $(\mathbf J_s,\mathbf M_s)$.
The theoretical contract is that the chosen stable boundary integral
formulation enforces transmission conditions and radiation without an
artificial outer boundary.

\subsection{Localized contrast-volume formulation}

A continuously varying constitutive region can be expressed through a
polarization/contrast current
\[
  \mathbf J_\Delta(\mathbf r)
  =
  j\omega[\epsilon(\mathbf r)-\epsilon_b]\mathbf E(\mathbf r)
  +[\sigma(\mathbf r)-\sigma_b]\mathbf E(\mathbf r).
\]
Only the support of the material contrast is discretized. The contrast basis
may be an adaptive point cloud, high-order local basis, or local volume
integral basis.

\begin{principle}[No false claim of zero discretization]
An arbitrary continuously varying three-dimensional constitutive law cannot in
general be solved exactly with a finite number of unknowns. Mesh-free in vNext
means that there is no global conforming volume mesh tied to public geometry;
localized quadrature points or compact basis expansions are permitted where the
material itself carries volume complexity.
\end{principle}

\subsection{Unified block operator}

All unknown coefficients are collected in
\[
  x_{\mathrm{EM}}
 =
 \begin{bmatrix}
  a_c & q_c & j_s & m_s & j_\Delta
 \end{bmatrix}^{\T}.
\]
For port excitation $i\in\C^P$,
\[
 \mathcal A_{\mathrm{EM}}(\mathcal S,\omega)
 x_{\mathrm{EM}}
 =
 \mathcal B_{\mathrm{EM}}(\mathcal S,\omega)i.
\]
The operator is evaluated matrix-free. Dense far interactions are accelerated
with a fast multipole or hierarchical kernel-compression backend
\cite{greengard1987fmm,hackbusch2015hierarchical}.

\subsection{Port extraction}

Let $\mathcal C_{\mathrm{EM}}$ extract electromotive response. Then
\[
  Z(\omega;\mathcal S)
 =
  Z_{\mathrm{local}}
  -\mathcal C_{\mathrm{EM}}\adj
   \mathcal A_{\mathrm{EM}}^{-1}
   \mathcal B_{\mathrm{EM}}.
\]
The local term contains analytically represented conductor/contact components
that should not be recovered by cancellation of two large numerical fields.
This Schur-complement view is the primary object learned by the neural
macromodel.
