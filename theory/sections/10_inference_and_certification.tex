\section{Online inference and residual certification}

\subsection{FAST path}

FAST inference performs:
\[
 \mathcal S
 \longrightarrow
 h_{\mathcal S}
 \longrightarrow
 \{\widehat{\mathcal R}_e,
   \widehat{\mathcal Q},
   \widehat{\mathcal R}_T\}.
\]
For narrowband operation the electrical port solve is a small dense circuit
solve. Thermal time evolution is an ODE in a small state $z$. Continuous loss
or temperature fields are evaluated only at requested query points.

No global Maxwell or thermal volume solve is executed in FAST mode.

\subsection{CERTIFIED electromagnetic path}

Let the learned/analytic field coefficients be $x_0$. Evaluate
\[
 r_0=b-\mathcal A_{\mathrm{EM}}x_0,\qquad
 \eta_0=\frac{\norm{r_0}}{\norm b}.
\]
If $\eta_0\le\epsilon_{\mathrm{accept}}$, accept the result. Otherwise perform a
bounded Krylov/recycling correction
\[
 \delta x_m\in
 \mathcal K_m(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{EM}}P^{-1},r_0),
 \qquad x_m=x_0+\delta x_m.
\]
The preconditioner may itself be learned, analytic, block-diagonal PEEC, or
hierarchical, but acceptance always uses the residual of the original physical
operator.

A certified prediction records
\[
 \{\eta_{\mathrm{EM}},
   \eta_{\mathrm{power}},
   m_{\mathrm{corr}},
   \text{quadrature policy}\}.
\]
If the correction budget is exhausted, the system either escalates to REFERENCE
mode or returns a non-certified status. It never silently downgrades accuracy.

\subsection{CERTIFIED thermal path}

For thermal evolution, certification combines:
\begin{enumerate}
\item positivity of $C_T$ and non-negative dissipation of $G_T$;
\item transfer-function residuals or selected REFERENCE impulse-response probes;
\item energy-balance residual over each accepted time step;
\item adaptive local truncation error in the $C_T$ energy norm.
\end{enumerate}
A scene that falls outside the trained thermal regime may construct an
on-demand compact thermal correction without changing public geometry
semantics.

\subsection{Latency targets}

The intended hierarchy is qualitative rather than hardware specific:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
Mode & Expensive operator solve & Intended use\\
\midrule
FAST & none & optimization, control, Monte Carlo, UI\\
CERTIFIED & few matrix-free corrections & high-accuracy engineering query\\
REFERENCE & converged teacher & offline truth and audit\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\subsection{OOD behavior}

Out-of-distribution detection is based on both learned uncertainty and physical
residuals. A large latent distance alone cannot invalidate a physically small
residual, and a familiar latent vector cannot override a large physical
residual. CERTIFIED mode therefore supplies the final authority.
