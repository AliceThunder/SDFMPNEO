\section{Structure-preserving neural operator}

\subsection{Role of learning}

The neural system is a correction and compression layer around explicit
physics. It is not a replacement for Maxwell's equations or heat diffusion.
The generic form is
\[
  \mathcal O(\mathcal S)
  =
  \mathcal O_{\mathrm{phys}}(\mathcal S)
  +\Delta\mathcal O_\theta(\mathcal S),
\]
followed by a structure-preserving decoder.

Analytic/current-filament/low-order PEEC models provide
$\mathcal O_{\mathrm{phys}}$. Learning focuses on finite-cross-section
corrections, many-body dielectric interactions, higher-order proximity effects,
and compact thermal transfer corrections.

\subsection{SE(3)-aware scene encoder}

Each object is encoded from intrinsic geometry and constitutive features.
Message passing uses relative poses $(R_{ij},d_{ij})$. The global latent must be
invariant to a common rigid transformation of the entire scene; local vector or
tensor features may transform equivariantly. A practical implementation may use
an $\SE(3)$-equivariant graph network or an invariant graph network with
explicit local frames.

The encoder must support a variable number of objects and materials. Padding a
scene to a fixed ordered parameter vector is not part of the theory.

\subsection{Global electromagnetic decoder}

The global head predicts factors of structured quantities rather than raw
matrix entries. Examples include
\[
 D_{\mathrm{abs}}=L_D L_D\adj,\qquad
 D_{\mathrm{out}}=L_O L_O\adj,
\]
and a reciprocal reactance parameterization
\[
 X=X^\T.
\]
At one frequency,
\[
 Z=D_{\mathrm{abs}}+D_{\mathrm{out}}+jX
\]
under the corresponding phasor/sign convention. For broadband models the head
predicts factors of the port-Hamiltonian realization in
Section~\ref{sec:port-macro}.

\subsection{Continuous field decoder}

Spatial loss and temperature decoders use a branch/trunk operator structure:
the scene encoder produces a global/object latent, while a query point is
expressed in object-local coordinates. The network returns coefficients or
factors of a continuous basis rather than a cell index. This is conceptually
related to operator-learning architectures such as DeepONet
\cite{lu2021deeponet}, but the vNext decoder is constrained by passivity,
positive loss, energy closure, and rigid-motion symmetry.

\subsection{Uncertainty and residual prediction}

A separate calibration head predicts expected physical residuals and epistemic
uncertainty from latent distance, ensemble disagreement, and cheap physics
features. This quantity never replaces a real residual in CERTIFIED mode; it
only decides whether FAST mode is appropriate and prioritizes active-learning
queries.

\subsection{Neural thermal model}

The thermal head predicts only structured reduced coefficients or corrections:
Cholesky factors of $C_T$, dissipative factors of $G_T$, source coupling
$B_T$, and continuous decoder coefficients. Stability is therefore independent
of the neural training loss.
