\section{Exact structure-preserving decoders}
\label{sec:structured-decoders}

This section specifies how learned latent variables are mapped to physical
electromagnetic and thermal operators. These maps are part of the model
definition, not post-processing repairs.

\subsection{Reciprocal narrowband impedance}

For a reciprocal $P$-port scene at a real frequency,
\[
  Z^\T=Z.
\]
Write
\[
  Z=R+jX,
\]
where reciprocity implies
\[
  R=R^\T\in\R^{P\times P},\qquad
  X=X^\T\in\R^{P\times P}.
\]
Passivity requires
\[
  R\PSD.
\]
The simplest exact decoder is therefore
\[
  R=L_RL_R^\T+\epsilon_R I,
  \qquad
  X=\operatorname{sym}(X_{\rm raw}),
\]
with $L_R$ real lower triangular and $\epsilon_R\ge0$ chosen according to
whether strict or semidefinite passivity is desired.

The model should normally predict a correction to a physical baseline rather
than the entire $R,X$. If the correction itself is not positive, the decoder
acts on the total structured quantity rather than projecting an arbitrary
predicted matrix afterward.

\subsection{Physical dissipative channels}

Individual channel operators
\[
 D_\alpha
\]
satisfy
\[
 D_\alpha=D_\alpha\adj\PSD
\]
because
\[
 \frac12 i\adj D_\alpha i
\]
is physical power in channel $\alpha$. A complex Hermitian channel matrix may
have imaginary off-diagonal entries even though the total reciprocal
dissipative matrix $R$ is real symmetric.

Therefore the decoder imposes
\[
  \sum_\alpha D_\alpha=R
  \label{eq:channel-closure}
\]
exactly.

One construction is:
\begin{enumerate}
\item predict raw PSD factors
  \[
    \widetilde D_\alpha=L_\alpha L_\alpha\adj;
  \]
\item form
  \[
    \widetilde D=\sum_\alpha\widetilde D_\alpha;
  \]
\item decode the total reciprocal dissipation $R$ independently as a real PSD
      matrix;
\item find a common congruence
  \[
    C=R^{1/2}\widetilde D^{\dagger/2};
  \]
\item set
  \[
    D_\alpha=C\widetilde D_\alpha C\adj.
  \]
\end{enumerate}
On the supported range this gives
\[
 \sum_\alpha D_\alpha=R
\]
while preserving channel PSD. Rank-deficient cases use a support-restricted
pseudoinverse plus a declared null-space allocation policy.

This is a low-dimensional port-space operation. Its correction magnitude is
reported during validation.

\subsection{Alternative simplex-of-PSD decomposition}

When the number of channels is fixed and small, learn contractive channel
factors $Q_\alpha$ satisfying
\[
 \sum_\alpha Q_\alpha Q_\alpha\adj=I
\]
on the support of $R$, then define
\[
 D_\alpha
 =
 R^{1/2}Q_\alpha Q_\alpha\adj R^{1/2}.
\]
The normalization can be constructed from unconstrained factors
$\widetilde Q_\alpha$ by the common inverse square root of
\[
 S=\sum_\alpha\widetilde Q_\alpha\widetilde Q_\alpha\adj.
\]
This parameterizes a matrix-valued partition of dissipative power.

\subsection{Continuous spatial loss closure}

Let raw continuous channel factors produce
\[
 \widetilde H_q(\xi)\PSD,
\]
with
\[
 \widetilde D_q
 =
 \int\widetilde H_q(\xi)\dd\mu(\xi).
\]
For the target absorbed operator $D_{\rm abs}$ define
\[
 C_q
 =
 D_{\rm abs}^{1/2}
 \widetilde D_q^{\dagger/2}.
\]
Then
\[
 H_q(\xi)
 =
 C_q\widetilde H_q(\xi)C_q\adj
\]
remains PSD and satisfies
\[
 \int H_q(\xi)\dd\mu(\xi)
 =
 D_{\rm abs}
\]
on the supported port subspace. Thus local positivity and integrated power
closure are exact simultaneously.

If the raw field has deficient support relative to $D_{\rm abs}$, decoding
fails closed; the model is not allowed to invent the missing port-power
direction through an arbitrarily large correction.

\subsection{Broadband electrical decoder}

For the first collocated passive state realization
($E_e=I$, $P_e=0$ in the general form of
Section~\ref{sec:port-macro}),
\[
 \dot x_e=(J_e-R_e)Q_e x_e+B_ei,
\]
decode
\[
 J_e=A_J-A_J^\T,
\]
\[
 R_e=L_RL_R^\T,
 \qquad
 Q_e=L_QL_Q^\T+\epsilon_QI,
\]
and a passive feedthrough
\[
 S_e=L_SL_S^\T.
\]
Reciprocity is imposed by a realization class whose transfer matrix is
symmetric. The exact realization constraints depend on the selected
port-Hamiltonian/circuit representation; arbitrary independent $B_e,C_e$ are
not used.

\subsection{Thermal decoder}

Decode
\[
 C_T=L_CL_C^\T+\epsilon_CI,
\]
\[
 G_T=L_GL_G^\T+G_{\rm env},
 \qquad G_{\rm env}\PSD.
\]
This guarantees Lyapunov stability of the zero-input thermal state under the
declared reference boundary conditions.

When the application requires a discrete maximum-principle-like guarantee
(non-negative temperature rise under non-negative heat), energy stability alone
is insufficient. The selected thermal realization must additionally be tested
for non-negative impulse responses at required observations, or use a positive
system realization where practical.

\subsection{No hidden projection}

Every normalization/congruence in this section is part of the declared decoder
and appears in training. Validation reports:
\[
 \eta_{\rm decode}
 =
 \frac{\norm{\text{raw physical quantity}-\text{decoded quantity}}}
      {\text{physical scale}}
\]
where meaningful. Large systematic decoder corrections indicate a failed neural
representation even if the final structured output is formally physical.
