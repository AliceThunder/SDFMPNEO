\section{Nondimensionalization, normalization, and immutable data contracts}
\label{sec:nondimensionalization}

Large scale disparities were a major source of conditioning and learning
difficulty in earlier architectures. vNext distinguishes physical units from
dimensionless numerical coordinates.

\subsection{Reference scales}

For each declared operating family choose positive reference scales
\[
 L_0,\quad \omega_0,\quad \sigma_0,\quad \epsilon_0^{\rm ref},
 \quad Z_0,\quad P_0,\quad T_0^{\Delta},\quad t_0 .
\]
Geometry is expressed as
\[
  \widetilde r=r/L_0,\qquad
  \widetilde\omega=\omega/\omega_0.
\]
One useful electromagnetic scale in inductive systems is selected from the
analytic baseline rather than hard-coded universally. Thermal time may use
\[
  t_0=L_0^2/\alpha_0
\]
for a representative diffusivity $\alpha_0$ when appropriate.

\subsection{Dimensionless operator equations}

The current--charge and thermal equations are scaled so all blocks have
comparable natural magnitude. If
\[
 x=S_x\widetilde x,\qquad
 b=S_b\widetilde b,
\]
then
\[
 \widetilde A
 =
 S_b^{-1}AS_x,\qquad
 \widetilde A\widetilde x=\widetilde b.
\]
The scaling matrices are deterministic functions of physical reference scales
and basis norms, not statistics fitted on train and validation together.

\subsection{Neural feature normalization}

Continuous shape parameters use dimensionless physical transforms:
length ratios, log-positive scales, bounded angle representations, and
constitutive ratios. Learned normalization statistics are computed only from
training data and stored in the artifact.

Rotations are never encoded as three raw Euler angles without periodicity
handling. Relative rotation matrices, quaternions with sign convention, or
equivariant tensors are preferred.

\subsection{Target normalization}

Structured matrix factors may vary over orders of magnitude. The model predicts
dimensionless factors relative to analytic baselines or training-only physical
scales. A normalized MSE is never interpreted as the final engineering error;
model selection uses decoded physical observables and residuals.

\subsection{Immutable scene sample}

Every training/reference sample has a content hash over:
\[
 \{\text{scene physics},
   \text{frequency/temperature domain},
   \text{teacher schema},
   \text{teacher tolerance},
   \text{output schema}\}.
\]
Training labels are immutable. Reprocessing a label with a new decoder or
normalization creates a derived dataset version rather than silently mutating
the physical truth.

\subsection{Dataset provenance}

A dataset manifest records:
\begin{enumerate}
\item theory/schema version;
\item generator commit and numerical backend versions;
\item deterministic scene seed or explicit scene description;
\item teacher tolerances and residual certificates;
\item split assignment fixed before training;
\item parent dataset and transformation history;
\item units and reference scales.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Training determinism}

Network initialization, scene batching, active-learning candidate generation,
and refit/replay seeds are explicit artifact metadata. Determinism is not a
substitute for statistical validation, but it is required to distinguish
algorithmic changes from random optimization variation.
