\section{Continuous electromagnetic loss operator}

\subsection{From cell tensors to a continuous operator}

The vNext thermal interface is not a vector of fixed-grid cell powers. For
every spatial point or material-local coordinate $\xi$, define a matrix factor
\[
  L_q(\xi;\mathcal S,\omega,T)\in\C^{r_q\times P}
\]
and the local Hermitian loss operator
\[
  H_q(\xi)=L_q(\xi)\adj L_q(\xi)\PSD.
\]
For port current $i$,
\[
  q(\xi;i)
  =\frac{1}{2}i\adj H_q(\xi)i
  =\frac{1}{2}\norm{L_q(\xi)i}_2^2
  \ge 0.
\]
Thus local non-negativity is exact by construction.

Different material objects use their natural measure: conductor volume,
dielectric volume, surface loss measure, or a reduced modal coordinate.
The global absorbed-power operator is
\[
  D_{\mathrm{abs}}
  =\int_{\Omega_{\mathrm{loss}}}
    H_q(\xi)\dd\mu(\xi).
\]

\begin{invariant}[Power closure]
For every port current,
\[
  \int q(\xi;i)\dd\mu(\xi)
  =\frac{1}{2}i\adj D_{\mathrm{abs}}i .
\]
The decoder enforces this identity structurally or by one final low-dimensional
congruence, never by redistributing heat on a world-grid stencil.
\end{invariant}

\subsection{Low-rank loss channels}

For thermal coupling, the continuous field is factorized as
\[
  H_q(\xi;\mathcal S)
  \approx
  \sum_{\ell=1}^{R_q}
  W_\ell(\mathcal S)\,
  \psi_\ell(\xi;\mathcal S),
\]
where $\psi_\ell$ are non-negative or PSD object-local basis fields transported
with geometry. Equivalently, instantaneous heat is represented by a small set
of source amplitudes
\[
  p_\ell(t)=p_\ell(i(t),x_e(t),T(t))
\]
and continuous source profiles
\[
  q(\mathbf r,t)
  =\sum_{\ell=1}^{R_q}p_\ell(t)\Psi_\ell(\mathbf r;\mathcal S,T).
\]
The rank $R_q$ is chosen by energy and downstream thermal observability, not by
the number of electromagnetic quadrature points.

\subsection{Conductor loss}

Conductor loss is computed directly from finite-cross-section modal currents:
\[
  q_c(\mathbf r)
  =\frac12 \Rea\{\mathbf J\adj\rho_e\mathbf J\},
\]
where $\rho_e=\sigma^{-1}$ may be tensor-valued and temperature dependent.
Because the conductor basis resolves skin and proximity modes, no separate
fine-minus-coarse self correction is part of the vNext theory.

\subsection{Dielectric and magnetic loss}

In a harmonic passive dielectric,
\[
  q_d
  =
  \frac{\omega}{2}
  \mathbf E\adj \Ima\{\epsilon\}\mathbf E
  +\frac{\omega}{2}
  \mathbf H\adj \Ima\{\mu\}\mathbf H ,
\]
with sign convention chosen consistently with the phasor convention. Equivalent
surface or contrast-volume unknowns are mapped to continuous loss factors
through the same integral representation used by the teacher.

\subsection{Spatial queries}

A user may request $q(\mathbf r)$ at arbitrary coordinates without retraining.
The query is transformed to relevant object-local coordinates and evaluated by
analytic basis functions plus a continuous neural residual. Spatial resolution
is therefore chosen at query time.
