\section{Thermal transfer operator and time evolution}

\subsection{Governing model}

The physical temperature field obeys
\[
 \rho_m c_p(T,\mathbf r)\frac{\pd T}{\pd t}
 -\nabla\cdot\big(k(T,\mathbf r)\nabla T\big)
 =q(\mathbf r,t)+q_{\mathrm{ext}}(\mathbf r,t),
\]
with interface continuity and the declared convection, radiation, or fixed
temperature boundaries.

The public thermal model is continuous in space and reduced in state. It must
not require construction of a fixed global thermal volume mesh for every query
geometry.

\subsection{Green and boundary-operator teacher}

For piecewise homogeneous linearized regions, Laplace transformation gives
\[
  \rho c\,s\,\widehat T-\nabla\cdot(k\nabla\widehat T)=\widehat q.
\]
In a homogeneous infinite region the transfer kernel has the characteristic
form
\[
  G_T(r,s)
  \propto
  \frac{\exp[-r\sqrt{s/\alpha}]}{4\pi k r},
  \qquad \alpha=\frac{k}{\rho c}.
\]
Interfaces are handled with thermal boundary integral operators. Time-domain
REFERENCE solutions may use convolution quadrature
\cite{lubich1988cq}. Localized continuously varying media may use adaptive
meshless/local volume corrections.

\subsection{Reduced stable state-space}

The thermal transfer from electromagnetic loss channels $p(t)\in\R^{R_q}$ to
temperature observations is reduced to
\[
 C_T(\mathcal S,\vartheta)\dot z
 =
 -G_T(\mathcal S,\vartheta)z
 +B_T(\mathcal S,\vartheta)p
 +b_{\mathrm{env}},
\]
\[
 T(\mathbf r,t)
 =
 T_{\mathrm{ref}}(\mathbf r)
 +\Phi_T(\mathbf r;\mathcal S)\,z(t),
\]
where $\vartheta$ denotes slowly varying nonlinear material coordinates.

We parameterize
\[
  C_T=L_C L_C^\T+\epsilon_C I,\qquad
  G_T=L_G L_G^\T+G_{\mathrm{bc}},
\]
with $\epsilon_C>0$ and $G_{\mathrm{bc}}\PSD$. This guarantees positive heat
capacity and passive diffusion.

\subsection{Continuous thermal decoder}

The decoder $\Phi_T(\mathbf r;\mathcal S)$ is expressed in object-local
coordinates plus background coordinates. A constant-temperature basis function
is included exactly, so a uniform initial temperature is representable without
projection error. The decoder may be evaluated only at requested observation
points, avoiding reconstruction of a global field when the user needs only
component maxima or selected probes.

\subsection{Nonlinear electrothermal coupling}

Temperature feeds back into
\[
 \sigma(T),\quad \epsilon(T),\quad \mu(T),\quad
 k(T),\quad c_p(T),
\]
and therefore into both the electromagnetic macromodel and thermal matrices.
The coupled reduced system is
\[
 \begin{aligned}
  \dot x_e &= f_e(x_e,i,z;\mathcal S),\\
  C_T(z)\dot z
    &=-G_T(z)z+B_T(z)p(x_e,i,z)+b_{\mathrm{env}},\\
  0&=F_{\mathrm{circ}}(v,i,x_c,u,t).
 \end{aligned}
\]
For narrowband WPT the carrier is eliminated algebraically and only the thermal
envelope is integrated. For broadband switching, $x_e$ remains a dynamic state.

\subsection{Time integration}

The reduced thermal system supports implicit, exponential, or IMEX methods.
Adaptive error control is performed in a physical energy norm induced by
$C_T$, not in arbitrary reduced coordinates:
\[
 \norm{\delta z}_{C_T}
 =\sqrt{\delta z^\T C_T\delta z}.
\]
Steady state is obtained from the same reduced operator and is accepted only if
the residual and local stability checks pass.
